PROMPTLY

UI/UX Design

2025

Overview

Promptly is a desktop website concept for writers who want a simple way to discover, organize, and develop creative ideas. The experience combines an approachable prompt library with a focused workspace for turning a spark into a story.

Screenshots & Mockups

Process

Research

  • Explored the needs of writers looking for a quick, low-friction way to overcome creative blocks.
  • Studied writing tools and prompt libraries to identify opportunities for clearer discovery and organization.
  • Defined a desktop-first experience that keeps inspiration and drafting close together.

Design

  • Created a clean, editorial interface that keeps prompts readable and the writing flow distraction-free.
  • Structured the experience around prompt discovery, saved ideas, and a dedicated writing workspace.
  • Designed a consistent visual system in Figma with clear hierarchy, spacious layouts, and purposeful interactions.
  • Used Photoshop to develop and refine the presentation mockups for the concept.

Outcome

  • Delivered a polished desktop website concept for writers and creative professionals.
  • Established a focused product direction that makes moving from idea generation to writing feel natural.
  • Created a foundation for future exploration of prompt collections, personalization, and collaborative writing.

Tools & Technologies

  • Vue
  • Vite
  • React
